# Alef proposal: "Every Classroom Has a Story" (authoritative)

Source: Google Doc "Alef_Telegram_Game_Proposal" (management proposal & game
design implementation guide, draft for discussion). This file records what the
build must follow. Where it conflicts with earlier docs, **this wins**.

## Direction
- Teacher-led classroom simulation; player helps **Ms. Mariam**. **No DLIS or
  mentor character required** (Toki/Ms. Moza are not needed in the teacher game).
- Goal: purposeful adoption of **NCLE (New Core Learning Experience)** tools;
  complements PD, does not replace it.
- Entry: the Alef Telegram Community post → Play. **No mandatory external links,
  channel exploration, secret codes or real-platform tasks during play.**
- Pilot **one mission first** before investing in a full game.

## Design rules
- Short, meaningful, accessible interactions; not a multiple-choice course.
- Reward investigation and purposeful decisions, not tapping or tech use.
- Randomness varies events, never arbitrarily judges teaching.
- Replay, pause, resume without penalties or streaks.
- The "five minutes" is narrative framing, **not a countdown**.

## Mission 01 — The First Five Minutes (MVP)
Grade 7, UAE public school. Ms. Mariam; Ahmed, Sara, Omar. 5–8 min.
Opening film (5 shots): arrival → students arrive → three students
(Ahmed: "Miss, I already know today's topic!" · Sara: "Miss, I still don't
understand what we learned yesterday." · Omar: "Miss, what are we doing
today?") → "Five minutes before we begin. Where should I start?" → camera
pulls back into the interactive classroom. Opening screen: "MISSION 01 | The
First Five Minutes · Your classroom. Your decisions. Your adventure. · Start
Playing". Hotspots: 3 students + 3 objects (laptop, board, bookshelf). No
fixed-ability labels.

- **Ahmed — The Confidence Detective**: 3 evidence cards (correct answer,
  explanation, application). One coherent profile chosen at encounter start:
  strong → extension, discover **Assign**, unlock *Beyond the Expected*;
  correct but weak reasoning → hidden misconception, questioning / **See Why**;
  difficulty applying prior knowledge → readiness, targeted support;
  strong + peer question → collaboration, **Teams** later.
- **Sara — Find the Missing Piece**: tap notebook, tablet, conversation bubble;
  consistent clues. Missing prerequisite → readiness support, *Bridging the
  Gap*; repeated misconception → **See Why**; needs task guidance → **Need
  Help** or **AI Helper**; understands but needs reinforcement → **Practice**.
- **Omar — What's Behind the Silence?**: distinguish unclear goal / missing prior
  knowledge / low confidence / distraction. Unclear goal → learning intention
  with **Present Mode**; low confidence → low-risk contribution or collaboration.
  A student response may reveal a misconception and open a new investigation.
- **Laptop — Build Your Learning Journey**: simplified, clearly labelled Alef
  simulation; arrange practice cards into Launch / Facilitate & Synthesize /
  Extend & Assess (drag-and-drop with tap alternative). Badge **Instructional
  Architect**. Sequence is illustrative; practices may recur across phases.
- **Board — Bring Learning to Life**: Present Mode or a prior-knowledge
  discussion; responses reveal starting points.
- **Bookshelf — Hidden discovery**: optional puzzle/collectible; cosmetic or
  educational; not required; limited repeats.

## Engine
Network of events. At each trigger: filter ineligible events, normalise
weights, pick one; none eligible → back to exploration or a milestone.
Illustrative weights: student learning 35 % · collaborative 25 % · unexpected
misconception 20 % · classroom management 15 % · rare bonus 5 %.
State: student (evidence revealed, need, support attempted, follow-up),
classroom (intention communicated, activity selected, discussion started,
evidence gathered), player (tools discovered, XP, events seen, progress, save).
Safeguards: coherent evidence profile per encounter; no XP farming; a
supportive choice may reveal more needs but is never "ineffective" because of
a random draw.

## Endings
- **Ready to Learn** (readiness checked, next steps identified) → badge
  Purposeful Launch; opens *Bring Learning to Life*.
- **The Unfinished Puzzle** (unresolved need) → opens *Bridging the Gap*.
- **An Unexpected Opportunity** (meaningful peer contribution) → opens
  *Everyone Has a Voice*.
Several eligible → prioritise the most relevant unresolved need, offer the
others as choices. Close on a milestone, not a click count.

## Rewards
Illustrative XP: 30 per investigation, 50 per first tool discovery, 100 for the
planning puzzle; once per meaningful action. Badges and cosmetic collectibles.
XP never equals teacher competence or student achievement. Cosmetic
customisation and optional competition are **later**, not MVP.

## NCLE content map (names to verify with product specialists)
Assign · Present Mode · Need Help / See Why / AI Helper / Practice · Teams /
Gamification (later) · Class Progress / Exit Ticket (later) · Show My Learning /
Exam Prep Assessment / Pathways (expansion).

## Measurement
Launch conversion, meaningful play (median active time, investigations,
decisions), replay/return, feature discovery (not use), adoption vs baseline
with approved data, voluntary teacher feedback. Fictional student data;
minimise teacher data; consent/retention documented.
