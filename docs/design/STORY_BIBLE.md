# Story bible — Toki's first term

A short film made of pictures plays between worlds: an **opening** the
first time a world is entered, a **closing** after its challenge. Each shot
is one full-screen still with a slow camera move; the narrator tells the
story, and when someone speaks their line pops up near them in their own
voice. Content lives in `src/data/story.ts`; the player is
`src/screens/StoryScreen.tsx`; stills are generated by
`.github/workflows/story.yml` → `tools/art/story.py`.

## Through-line

Toki starts his first term as an instructional coach. His mentor,
**Ms. Moza** (a senior Emirati coach), gives him an empty notebook and one
rule: *"Don't write what you see. Write what they learn."* Each world adds
one lens to the notebook. In World 7 Moza meets him again at the Academy,
where the hardest skill is knowing when nothing is wrong. The Final World is
the Master DLIS visit — a whole school, every lens at once — and the film
ends with the notebook full and Toki back on the road, asking the same
question everywhere: *is it still doing what it was for?*

| World | Opening (shots) | Lens Toki learns (closing) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Busy Classroom | 5 — gate, Moza's notebook, busy corridor, Ms. Mariam's room, the blank page | Activity is not the same as learning (2) |
| 2 Assessment | 3 — charts everywhere, Mr. Rashid's tickets, the unchanged plan | A test is a question for the teacher (1) |
| 3 Alef in Action | 3 — green dashboards, Mr. Hamad's 100 %, "What did you figure out?" | Technology should make thinking louder (1) |
| 4 Feedback | 3 — Ms. Latifa's praise, Ms. Alya's late-night marking, "What did I get?" | Feedback works when students act on it (1) |
| 5 Questioning | 3 — same three hands, one-second wait, the quiet thinkers | A good question gives everyone time (1) |
| 6 Differentiation | 3 — bronze/silver/gold, September groups, the Sparrow who's ready | Different tasks ≠ different support; the Academy invitation (2) |
| 7 Academy | 4 — the flawless school, Moza's warning, Ms. Hessa's quiet room, the magnifying glass | Sometimes the finding is "nothing is wrong"; "You're ready." (2) |
| 8 Master DLIS | 3 — one school, the leadership's "Where do we start?", every lens at once | A habit, not a checklist; the full notebook (2) |

38 shots, 9 spoken quotes. All characters are fictional; teachers are the
game's own (Mariam, Rashid, Hamad, Latifa, Alya, Huda, Hessa); Moza,
Sara (a student) and Mr. Jasim (a principal) are new.

## Writing rules

- Narration: one or two short sentences, calm adult register, present tense.
  Arabic is Gulf-friendly MSA, never a calque.
- Quotes ≤ 12 words, things a real person would say.
- Every opening ends on a question the world answers; every closing names
  the lens in plain words.
- The film never judges a teacher; it shows what Toki notices.

## Pictures

- **Style** comes from the art director's preamble (`docs/design/ART_BIBLE.md`,
  `tools/art/gemini.py`). `tools/art/story.py` adds a film-still brief:
  portrait 2:3 that fills a phone, subject in the centre band, warm painterly
  light, modest UAE school setting, Toki matching the reference art, **no
  text anywhere in the image** (captions and quotes are drawn by the game in
  both languages).
- Shots with a quote ask for calm space on the speaker's side for the pop-up;
  shots without one keep a darker band at the bottom for subtitles.
- Missing stills show an illustrated placeholder, so the film works before
  generation runs.

## Voice

Narration and quotes are ordinary lines in the main voice pipeline
(`src/audio/storyLines.ts`, exported by `tools/audio/export-script.ts`):
narrator slot for narration, Toki's slot for his lines, a teacher slot chosen
from the speaker's name for everyone else, with an acting `mood`. The player
paces each shot to the voice and falls back to reading time when voice is off.
