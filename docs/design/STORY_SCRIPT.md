## Assessment Under the Microscope

- **Narrator (EN):** Mr. Rashid collects the exit tickets as the bell goes — every lesson, without fail.

- **Mr. Rashid says (AR):** «كل بطاقة، كل يوم.»
- **Picture:** A science classroom at the end of a lesson: Mr. Rashid, an Emirati male teacher in a white kandura and ghutra, holds a perfectly squared stack of small paper slips, students filing out behind him. Proud, organised.

**Shot 3** · `w2-open-3` · ✅

- **Narrator (AR):** يلتقط توكي واحدة. أحد عشر طالبًا أخطؤوا في السؤال نفسه. وخطة الغد لم تتغيّر.
- **Picture:** Close-up: Toki holding up a single paper slip to the light, studying it; on the desk beside him a printed lesson plan sits untouched. Thoughtful mood, side lighting.

### Closing

**Shot 1** · `w2-close-1` · ✅

- **Narrator (AR):** الاختبار سؤال للمعلّم، لا للطالب وحده.
- **Picture:** Toki and Mr. Rashid side by side at a desk, pinning one exit ticket onto the next day’s lesson plan with a paperclip, both smiling slightly. Morning light, collaborative mood.
