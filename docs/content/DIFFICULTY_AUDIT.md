# Difficulty audit — Worlds 2–6 (content pass, Nov 2026)

**Why this pass.** Kandil's verdict on v2: "very basic and easy". For Worlds 2–6 the cause was mostly in the content, not the engine. Three leaks made the correct option guessable without reading the evidence:

1. **Position.** The correct drift option was first in 19 of 20 classrooms; the correct coaching move was second in 20 of 20; the reasoning answer was always `r2` and always second.
2. **Length and register.** The correct option was the longest in 52 of 60 steps, and the only one phrased in coaching language ("…what students understood, struggled with, and learned"). Distractors were strawmen ("Stop using exit tickets", "Reward the fastest finishers", "Praise and leave") that no DLIS would pick.
3. **Bosses.** Healthy scenes looked obviously healthy and pattern scenes announced themselves ("Nobody mentions a student by name… nobody's plan is different").

**What changed.** 180 options rewritten (20 classrooms × 3 steps × 4 options = 240, of which 60 correct options were kept in meaning and trimmed; plus 5 bosses × 2 steps × 4 = 40 options). All ids and `correctId`s preserved; only positions moved. Every distractor is now a move a competent coach could defend for ten seconds — it fixes the symptom, is right in a neighbouring classroom, or treats the wrong drift. Hints after a wrong answer now point at a specific evidence clue instead of restating the answer. Ten boss scenes rewritten so that in every boss at least one healthy scene looks worse on the surface than a pattern scene.

## For the pedagogy team to review

These are judgement calls I made that a DLIS lead should confirm before rollout:

1. **25 (boss) coaching** — I made "require every assessment in the dashboard within 24 hours" a distractor. Some schools run exactly that policy; the game now says it speeds up reporting without changing teaching. Confirm this matches Alef's stance.
2. **35 (boss) coaching** — "change the completion target from 97% to 85%" is a distractor. If the Pedagogy Hub actively recommends lowering completion targets, soften this one.
3. **43 coaching** — "comments first, grade a week later" is a well-known technique (Butler, 1988) and is now a *wrong* answer because the level's drift is use, not reading. Players who know the research may object; the hint explains the distinction. Keep or swap for a weaker distractor.
4. **54 coaching** — "mini whiteboards, all boards up together" is now a distractor. It is close to a wait-time routine in practice. The hint argues it still fires on the signal; a reviewer may prefer it as a second acceptable answer (the engine supports only one).
5. **55 (boss) scene "Grade 6"** — the healthy teacher now has a 2/5 on file from the school's own rubric. This is deliberate and pointed; confirm it will not read as a dig at any real observation rubric in use (the Al Ain T-Obs rubric scores questioning differently).
6. **All Arabic** — rewritten in a coaching register, not literal; ~250 new Arabic strings in this pass alone. A native DLIS should read Worlds 2–6 end to end in Arabic before the pilot.

## Not changed on purpose

- Risk options stay in fixed severity order (minor → lethal); shuffling them would be hostile, not hard.
- `correctId`s unchanged everywhere, so existing saves and badges are unaffected.
- No engine or type changes; the pass is data only. World 1, World 7 and the Final World are owned by other branches.
